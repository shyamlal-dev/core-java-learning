## Interview Questions and Answers

### 1. What is a constructor?

A constructor initializes a new object and has the same name as the class.

### 2. What is a default constructor?

It is a no-argument constructor generated by the compiler when the class declares no constructor.

### 3. Is every no-argument constructor a default constructor?

No. A no-argument constructor may be explicitly written. A default constructor is generated by the compiler.

### 4. Can constructors be inherited or overridden?

No. Constructors belong to their declaring classes and are not inherited.

### 5. Why must `this()` or `super()` be the first statement?

Parent or delegated construction must complete before the current constructor continues initialization.

### 6. What happens if `super()` is not written?

The compiler inserts a no-argument `super()` call if another constructor call is not present.

### 7. Can a constructor be private?

Yes. A private constructor restricts object creation and is used in utility classes, factories, and singleton designs.

### 8. Why should overridable methods not be called from constructors?

The child override may execute before the child object's fields are fully initialized, producing inconsistent behavior.

### 9. Can an abstract class have a constructor?

Yes. Its constructor initializes the abstract-class portion of child objects.

### 10. Can an interface have a constructor?

No. Interfaces cannot be instantiated and do not maintain instance construction state.
